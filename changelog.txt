---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
-- Cataclysm (planet mod for Factorio Space Age)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-08-30
  Changes:
    - Full playable planet "Cataclysm" beyond Aquilo (space connection aquilo-cataclysm).
    - Map generation: Aquilo-style noise (islands, starting ore spots, storm condensate lakes).
    - Natural risk: constant lightning (Fulgora-style) + scripted superstorm cycles (period setting).
    - Resources: stormite ore, astrite ore (deep/rare); fluids: storm condensate, charged condensate.
    - Machines: condensate extractor, storm siphon, storm foundry, storm fabricator, storm generator.
    - Production loop: condensate -> charging (lightning) -> charged condensate -> power + science.
    - New science pack: cataclysmic science pack (added to labs with cryogenic input).
    - 12 technologies, 11 achievements (7 native + 4 scripted).
    - Decoratives: crystal trees, cracked rocks, storm vents, ancient spires (spire reward).
    - Localization: en, ru, de (all entities/recipes/techs/achievements/messages).
    - Procedural graphics (PIL) for all icons, tech/achievement icons, ore sheets, decoratives.
    - Static checks: tools/check_lua.py (syntax, graphics refs, locale parity, recipe unlock),
      tools/check_icons.py (PNG integrity/sizes).
    - Additive vanilla change only: cataclysmic science pack added to lab inputs.
    - Optional PlanetsLib integration (data-updates): orbit-tree registration
      (planet moves with Aquilo), borrowed Aquilo music, default import location.
    - Dependency study documented in docs/DEPENDENCIES.md (PlanetsLib, flib, stdlib).
    - Release packaging: tools/make_release.py -> release/cataclysm_0.1.0.zip.
